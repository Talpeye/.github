<p align="center"><strong>Keep your operation moving.</strong></p>

Talpeye is a maintenance system for industrial plants. It puts machine health, spare parts and technician schedules in one place, so a team knows what needs fixing, what the job needs, and when to do it.

A diagnosis only helps once it becomes a date. Talpeye chooses that date, weighing the risk of waiting against the production a stop would cost, with parts and people as the limits. The plan stays current as readings arrive. Every change a person makes is kept with the reason behind it.
